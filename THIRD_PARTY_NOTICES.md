# Third-party visual assets

Exercise illustrations displayed by this site are loaded from **Workout Guide** by Bryl Lim:

- Project: https://github.com/bryllim/workout-guide
- Gallery: https://bryllim.github.io/workout-guide/
- Visual asset license: **CC BY-SA 4.0**
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Workout Guide states that its original pose artwork builds on Everkinetic artwork under CC BY-SA 4.0 and that Bryl Lim expanded the library with additional exercises and frames. The site displays the original Workout Guide SVG frames without redrawing their geometry.

No visual is intentionally substituted for a merely similar exercise. When a precise/equivalent open visual has not been mapped, the site shows the text instruction without an illustration instead.
